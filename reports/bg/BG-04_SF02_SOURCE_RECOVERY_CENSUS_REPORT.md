## Recovered documentary evidence

### P079 — Mediterranean / Orient hypothesis

Primary source trail: `WhatsApp Chat with +39 333 652 7693(2).txt`, 02 Jun 2026 around 14:50.

Recovered content establishes:
- the map is historical/cultural and hypothetical, not proof;
- Mesopotamia/Assyria/Babylon/Persian world/Near East precede the Greco-Epirote/Roman discussion;
- proposed path: Mesopotamia/Nineveh → Persia/Iran → Anatolia/Eastern Mediterranean → Epirus/Molossians → Rome → Southern Italy/Apulia;
- explicit statement that this is not demonstrated genealogy.

### P081 — Rome / provinces

Primary source trail: same WhatsApp corpus, route discussion around the Roman provincial-map sequence.

Recovered content establishes:
- legions, roads, colonies, villas/latifundia, trade and ports as transmission mechanisms;
- routes from Rome to Southern Italy/Apulia, Gallia, Hispania, Africa Proconsularis, Illyricum/Dalmatia, Epirus/Greece, Asia Minor/Anatolia, Britannia;
- local adaptation rather than unchanged transmission of a modern breed.

**Locked-target caution:** the accepted visual target visibly uses the number `7` for both `EPIRO / GRECIA` and `ASIA MINORE / ANATOLIA`, followed by `8 BRITANNIA`. This is not to be silently normalized during source recovery. The direct-master target remains the visible-source authority unless an exact native final source proves a different accepted state.

### P083 — Italy functional development

Recovered correspondence establishes:
- comparison of Cane Corso, Mastino Napoletano and local functional traditions;
- Dogo Sardesco, Bucceriscu Calabrese and Vecceriscu/Vucciriscu Siciliano as distinct remembered local/functional types rather than identities to merge;
- emphasis on work, territory, selection and functional continuity;
- caution against reducing the historical Cane Corso to only one modern regional interpretation.

The exact target wording around `VARIAZIONE DI MOLE, NON DI FUNZIONE`, the `tipo pesante / normotipo` caution and the final compact panel structure was not recovered as page-exact documentary prose. Those units therefore derive canonically from the locked target.

### P085 — Balkans / Thrace / Bulgaria

Recovered correspondence explicitly describes a visual hypothesis for today’s Bulgarian Roman lands:
- Moesia Inferiore;
- Thrace;
- Danubian limes;
- Roman roads;
- Balkan passes;
- villages, villae rusticae and local communities;
- guard, livestock, castra, roads/frontiers and everyday protective work;
- explicit statement that this is a study question, not documented breed/genealogy.

City labels such as Oescus, Novae, Serdica, Philippopolis, Marcianopolis and Odessos are final-target map content; no exact documentary page-definition file was recovered for them.

### P087 / P090 — comparative world atlas / methodological master

Recovered correspondence explicitly establishes:
- a world map/atlas of molossoid/strong functional traditions;
- Italy, France, Spain/UK, Balkans, Turkey/Anatolia, Caucasus, Central Asia, Tibet, Japan, South America, South Africa and other regions;
- no complete scientific classification and no proven genealogy;
- the idea that different environments and human functions can produce comparable strong/protective types;
- distinction between recognized modern breeds and older/local types or traditions.

Flavio’s direct correction is also recovered: ancient/global discussion should not flatten everything into modern “breeds”; selection is historically tied to function, and domestic breeds result from human selection, mutation and crossing.

### P091 / P092 / P093 — European, Balkan/Caucasus/Anatolian/Central-Asian and Tibetan context

Flavio source corpus directly establishes:
- Mastino Abruzzese, Pyrenean mountain dog, Spanish mastiff, Estrela/Castro Laboreiro;
- Anatolian Akbash/Karabash/Kangal;
- Ciarplanina/Šarplanina region;
- Caucasian and Central Asian shepherd traditions;
- environment, mountain climate, predators, herd protection and adaptation as drivers of similar morphology/function;
- Tibetan mastiff as one geographically/localized expression of a livestock-guardian tradition, not an automatic universal progenitor.

This is strong provenance for P092 and the Tibetan part of P093. The exact China/Japan/Tosa wording remains direct-target authority.

### P094 / P095 — Africa / Americas

The recovered 02 Jun world-atlas correspondence explicitly names South Africa and South America and defines the comparison as functional/environmental, not a common demonstrated bloodline.

No page-exact documentary source was recovered for the final regional paragraphs, modern-type examples, North/Central/Southern-Cone subdivisions or closing aphorisms. Those final units therefore derive from the direct-master targets.

A later Flavio correction in the correspondence states that `molosso` is too generic as a modern typological label and specifically notes that Dogo Argentino is a polyhybrid. This is relevant historical/editorial context for later audit, but it does **not** authorize silently rewriting the accepted P095 Italian source. P095 itself is framed as modern selection/migration and rejects a single ancestral line.

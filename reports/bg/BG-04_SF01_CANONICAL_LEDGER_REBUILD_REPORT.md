## Source-policy decisions

### P011 — publication titles
Publication titles, author spellings, publishers, years, and the photographed/published covers remain original bibliographic evidence. Bulgarian translation is applied only to editorial headings/notes, not by inventing Bulgarian publication titles.

### P044 — functional hunting categories
`Sagaces`, `Veleres`, and `Pugnax` are retained as historical/Latin category terms inside Bulgarian wording. `segugi` and `levrieri` are translated as `гончета` and `хрътки`.

### P048 — terminology clarification
`Vucceri` is retained as a dialect term. `cane da presa` is retained visibly and accompanied by a Bulgarian functional gloss rather than being reduced to the word `ухапване`. The underlying source material explains `presa` as targeted grasping, holding, and immobilization.

### P061 — final target wins
`Tipi .2.png` is corroboration only. The locked target governs every cell. In particular, the target reads `Mastino Abruzzese, Kuvaz, Komondor` and excludes the `Bovaro Bernese` present in the recovered variant.

### P070 — source fidelity
The source graphic's claims and role line are translated as visible source content; this canonical ledger does not silently rewrite or fact-correct the source graphic.

### P076 — Pinelli caution
The poster wording is translated, while the separate final-book page caption that says the image is a contemporary study reconstruction remains a separate page-level production element and is not absorbed into the graphic ledger.

### P078 — hypothesis status
The Bulgarian target preserves the graphic's methodological caution: questions before answers, comparisons before conclusions, hypotheses rather than genealogies, and verification as necessary.

### P098 — proper names and routes
`Il Contado del Molise`, `Molise`, and named tratturi/routes are retained as names; descriptive headings are translated.

### P121 — documentary photograph
The real sign is treated as documentary evidence. Its internal Italian sign text is retained inside the photograph rather than repainted into Bulgarian. The canonical ledger therefore uses `R` for its visible sign text and a retained microtext block. Any Bulgarian explanation belongs in a separate editorial caption, not inside the historical/documentary photograph.

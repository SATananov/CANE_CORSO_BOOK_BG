## 1. Mandatory global rules

1. Use the approved Bulgarian form exactly as listed. Inflect Bulgarian common nouns naturally; do not alter locked proper names.
2. At first occurrence of a retained historical, regional, Latin, or institutional term, add the Bulgarian explanation shown here when context permits. Later occurrences may use the retained form alone.
3. Do not convert similarity, continuity, functional analogy, or a historical name into proof of direct ancestry.
4. Preserve five distinct evidentiary/voice levels:
   - **established historical fact** — state directly only when the source presents it as documented;
   - **historical hypothesis** — use markers such as „хипотеза“, „възможно“, „вероятно“, „би могло“;
   - **interpretation** — use „тълкуване“, „прочит“, „интерпретация“;
   - **Dott. Flavio Bruno’s personal position** — attribute as „според д-р Флавио Бруно“, „Флавио смята/подчертава/предлага“;
   - **Stefano De Tanini’s editorial narrative** — attribute as „Стефано Де Танини разказва/обяснява/съпоставя“ or retain first-person editorial voice where the source does.
5. Never replace an attribution with an impersonal factual assertion.
6. Latin expressions remain Latin. Supply a Bulgarian explanation only if the source supplies one or the sentence requires comprehension; never present the explanation as part of the original quotation.
7. Original publication titles and bibliographic data remain in their published language. Bulgarian renderings may be added in square brackets only as editorial aids and must not imply an existing Bulgarian edition.
8. Labels in tables, diagrams, and infographics use the same locked terms as body text. Do not invent shortened synonyms to fit cells.
9. `MANUAL DECISION REQUIRED` blocks use of that entry until the owner decides. No such unresolved entry may be guessed during BG-03.
